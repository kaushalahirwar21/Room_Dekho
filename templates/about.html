About Room Dekho

Room Dekho is a room rental platform that helps people find verified rooms, flats, hostels, and PGs directly from property owners without paying brokerage. Our mission is to make room searching simple, transparent, and hassle-free.

Our Mission

To connect tenants with property owners through a secure and easy-to-use platform while eliminating unnecessary brokerage fees and making room hunting faster.

Why Choose Room Dekho

Verified Listings

Every room listing is carefully verified to ensure the images, location, and details match reality.

No Brokerage

Say goodbye to heavy broker fees. Talk directly to the owner and save your hard-earned money.

Direct Owner Contact

Instantly get the owner's mobile number and start negotiating or book a physical room visit.
